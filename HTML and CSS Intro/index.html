The Apothecary Diaries - Anime Review

Samya El Boghdadi | 5/6/2026

Best anime ever!!

This anime is a historical drama based on the light novel of the same name. It follows the main character Maomao, a palace maid who is a skilled apothecary. I thoroughly enjoyed this show, and even binged both seasons in less than a week.
The character designs, storylines, animation, and soundtrack were all amazing and nothing felt lacking. I am eagerly waiting for the next season to come out soon!
